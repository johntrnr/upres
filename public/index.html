Replicate account

Upres runs SeedVR2, FLUX and Nano Banana Pro on Replicate with your own token. Create one at replicate.com/account/api-tokens, and add credit or a card at replicate.com/account/billing.

The token stays on this device. It is only sent to your own Upres server, which forwards it to Replicate. Runs are billed to your Replicate account.
